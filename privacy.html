1. Who we are and what this covers

Paintivo is a colouring and painting app developed by Pir Ali Haider Chishti. This policy covers the current Android app, package com.pk.ahfarid.art.paint, and these information pages. Our privacy contact is ali.haider7861234@gmail.com.

The app does not currently offer user accounts, public artwork posting, developer-operated cloud backup, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. If data practices change, this notice will be updated before the new practices are introduced.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Artwork and progress: editable colouring layers, completed images, thumbnails, selected page identifiers, completion status, and save timestamps are stored in the app’s private storage. This lets you continue a draft and view My Artworks.
  • Gallery copies: if you choose “Finish & save to phone,” a composed PNG is written to your phone’s Pictures/Paintivo location. The developer does not receive the picture through this export.
  • Age group: a birth year is used temporarily to choose Child, Teen, or Adult. Paintivo saves only that group, not the entered year or exact age, and does not log the birth year. Approximate classification may place you in the younger group.
  • Preferences and ad timing: local settings include the age group and the time of the last full-screen ad. Google’s consent component may separately store consent choices on the device.
  • Downloaded content: installed picture packs and a cached catalogue are saved locally when the optional download service is configured and you use it.

Paintivo does not upload drawings, gallery images, or the entered birth year to a developer-operated server. If you email us an attachment or share an exported image through another app, that is a separate action you choose.

3. Google advertising and consent services

Paintivo integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for banners and occasional interstitial ads, and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy and consent messages. There are no app-open ads. Ads are not displayed on the painting canvas.

Google documents that its advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses, app/ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. An IP address can be used to estimate general location. Data supports ad delivery, measurement, service operation, analytics, and fraud prevention; actual processing depends on the SDK, device settings, age restrictions, and consent configuration.

The age policy is applied before ad initialization and requests. Ads are requested only when the consent component permits them. Adult ad personalization is subject to applicable consent choices; child and teen requests are marked non-personalized and age-restricted. Paintivo disables Google’s publisher first-party ID setting after SDK initialization, before ad requests.

Important: non-personalized ads can still require network requests and process information. An age-group choice is not a promise that only gaming ads will appear and is not itself consent to every form of processing.

Google processes information under its own policies. Read Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information, and the Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Google and its service providers may process data in countries other than your own.

4. Children and teenagers

Paintivo can be used by different age groups. We encourage a parent or guardian to review this policy and supervise younger users. The current app asks for a year rather than a complete birthday and retains only the resulting age group. Closing the age entry without a valid year uses the more restrictive child setting.

Child requests use child-directed treatment and a G maximum ad content rating. Teen requests use under-age treatment and a PG maximum rating. Both use non-personalized ad requests. These are technical request settings, not a guarantee about every creative or a certification that all legal requirements have been met.

We do not ask children to create an account, provide an email address, or send a drawing to use the painting features. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information that should not have been provided, contact us so we can review and delete information we control where appropriate. Please do not send the child’s exact birthday or an identity document.

5. Optional picture downloads

The bundled library works offline. The optional “Download more” client requests a catalogue, pack manifests, and PNG files only when you use its download or refresh controls. In the current configuration, the catalogue address is empty, so the feature does not contact a picture host.

If this service is activated, files are requested over HTTPS from the configured host. The host receives normal request information such as your IP address, requested file address, time, and network/client headers, and may keep access or security logs. Paintivo does not include your entered birth year or drawings in those requests.

The host and any additional data practices will be named in this policy before the service is activated. Downloads are verified before installation and stored in the app’s private content-pack folder. They do not create a developer account or automatically upload your artwork.

6. Device permissions and access

  • Internet access: used for advertising, consent checks, and optional configured picture downloads. Bundled colouring and local saving do not require an internet connection.
  • Gallery export on Android 8–9: Paintivo requests storage-write permission when you export a picture. Denial does not remove your private saved artwork; you can grant permission later and retry.
  • Gallery export on Android 10 or later: Paintivo uses Android’s MediaStore to create its own PNG without requesting broad read access to your photo collection.

Paintivo’s painting features do not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location. Advertising services can still infer general location from network information without a location permission.

7. Support email and these web pages

If you email ali.haider7861234@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue, and manage related correspondence. Gmail processes email under Google’s policies. Do not send passwords, unnecessary personal information, or sensitive details about a child.

These static pages do not include our own advertising tags, analytics scripts, forms, cookies, or browser-storage code. Fonts and images are served with the pages rather than requested from a separate font provider.

When these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes ordinary connection and request information as the hosting provider. Its practices are described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Clicking an external link takes you to a service with its own policy.

8. Retention and deletion

Private app data: artwork and installed packs remain on the device until they are removed through available app/device controls or the app’s storage is cleared. To remove the app’s local artwork, content packs, and preferences together, use Android Settings → Apps → Paintivo → Storage → Clear storage/data. This is destructive; export any pictures you want to keep first.

Exported pictures: a gallery PNG is separate from private app data. Delete it through your Gallery/Photos or file-manager app. Clearing Paintivo’s storage does not necessarily delete those exported copies.

Device backups: backup or restore performed by Android or another device service depends on your device and backup settings. Manage any such copies with that service; Paintivo does not provide its own cloud-sync service.

Support correspondence: we retain messages only for as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up or meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete correspondence we control by emailing us. We cannot directly access or remotely delete private drawings on your phone.

Provider records: Google, GitHub, and email/backup providers manage their own retention and deletion under their policies. Removing local app data does not automatically delete their server-side records. Paintivo currently has no app account to close.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

In Paintivo, open Home → About → Privacy & ads to review the selected age group. “Consent options” opens Google’s privacy options when available or required for your session. Those options can vary by region and configuration. Use “Change age group” to correct the group if needed. These choices affect advertising, not access to saved pictures or colours.

You can manage app permissions, advertising/privacy settings, and backups in your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability for personal information we actually hold, and to complain to a relevant data protection authority. Contact us to ask about those rights. We may request only the information necessary to identify and handle the request.

For data held directly by Google or another provider, use that provider’s privacy controls or request process. Merely reading this policy does not grant advertising consent.

10. Security and policy changes

Drawings are stored in app-private storage unless you export them. Picture-pack transfers use HTTPS and integrity checks before installation. No device, network, or email system can be guaranteed completely secure, so protect your device and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information to support.

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change and revise its “Last updated” date. Where a change requires additional disclosure or consent, it must be presented before the relevant processing starts. Check this policy alongside the version of Paintivo you use.
