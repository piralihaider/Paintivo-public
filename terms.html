4. Local saving, export, and content downloads

Editable drafts and completed pictures are stored on your device. Gallery export produces a separate image file. Export and saving may fail because of insufficient storage, denied permission, device issues, or interruption; pay attention to the app’s status messages.

Keep separate copies of pictures that are important to you. Clearing app data, removing the app, resetting a device, or losing a device may remove private artwork. Paintivo does not currently provide developer-operated cloud backup or cross-device account synchronization.

The bundled library can be used offline. Additional picture packs are available only if a catalogue has been published and configured. Downloading may use your internet connection and mobile-data allowance. You can cancel an active download; failed transfers may need to be retried from the beginning.

Download availability, catalogue content, and device compatibility may change. These terms do not promise a particular number of future pictures or continuous access to an unconfigured download service.
